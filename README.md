# Hi, I'm Tejaswini 👋

**Software Engineer at Microsoft** — Azure, Intune & PKI, working on the cloud services and certificate diagnostics behind endpoint security at fleet scale. Previously a Senior Software Engineer at Intel (IoT) and an R&D Software Engineer (AI). M.S. Computer Science, UT Dallas.

These days my curiosity runs in one direction: **what happens when AI systems have to be trusted in the real world — evaluated, gated, and still running years from now, in homes that outlive the people who set them up.**

## What I'm building

- **PilotGate** — a production-style LLM evaluation & safety system: golden-set scoring, a safety probe battery, and cost metering, built the way I'd want any model gated before it touches users.
- **Below the Cap** — a reflective companion grounded entirely in my own writing. It translates complaints into the need underneath them, guides short nervous-system regulation practices, and mirrors journal patterns back — with an evaluation that grades it honestly, failures included.
- **The Last Administrator** — research on a question nobody designs for: what happens to a smart home when its only administrator dies or loses capacity? A taxonomy of five orphaned-device states, and the design requirements for homes that survive their owners.
